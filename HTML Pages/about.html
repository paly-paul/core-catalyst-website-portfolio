The problem we saw

Skills data exists. It just doesn't agree with itself.

"Every HRIS said something different about the same person's skills. We wanted to know who was ready for what - and nobody could tell us."

The team behind CoreCatalyst spent years embedded in large enterprise HR functions - as consultants, as practitioners, as system architects. The pattern was always the same: organizations had skill data in abundance, scattered across Workday, LinkedIn, LMS platforms, performance reviews, and manager notes. None of it matched. None of it was trusted.

When a CHRO wanted to know if the organization had the skills to navigate an AI transition, the answer took three months and two off-cycle surveys to produce. When a manager wanted to know who on their team was ready for a stretch role, they picked up the phone and asked around. When HR Ops tried to keep Workday current, they ran manual reconciliation sprints that consumed entire weeks.

The root cause wasn't lack of data. It was conflict. The same person had a different skill profile in every system they'd ever touched - and there was no engine to resolve the contradiction.

In 2023, the yourskills.ai team started building that engine. Not a survey tool. Not another profile. A reconciliation layer that sits beneath your existing systems, absorbs their conflicting signals, applies a 45,000-node taxonomy and a weighted conflict-resolution model, and writes a single trusted skills record back to your HRIS.

CoreCatalyst is that engine, packaged for enterprise HR teams who need answers in weeks - not months - and who can't afford to ask their workforce to fill in yet another form.

How we got here

2021
The insight Founding team identifies the skill reconciliation gap while advising a 12,000-person enterprise on a Workday migration. Three source systems, zero agreement on what anyone could do.
2022
yourskills.ai launches The individual-facing layer goes live: a personal skills passport that aggregates credentials, work history, and assessments into one verified profile. Early signs that the employer side has an even bigger problem.
2023
The enterprise pivot After 60+ conversations with HR leaders, a clear pattern: the problem isn't the employee's profile - it's the organization's inability to reconcile signals across systems. CoreCatalyst R&D begins. The 45K-node taxonomy is seeded.
2024
First enterprise pilots Three design partners go live. 94% auto-resolution rate on first run. HRIS write-back to Workday ships. The pattern holds: two weeks to first report, significant reduction in manual reconciliation hours.
2025
CoreCatalyst GA General availability with three-tier pricing. Board reporting module ships. SAP and Oracle write-back connectors added. The team grows to 28 people across product, engineering, and customer success.
What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on

These aren't wall posters. They're the decisions we make when trade-offs get hard.
🔎

Truth over tidiness

We'd rather surface a conflict than paper over it. Skill data that disagrees with itself is a fact to be resolved, not a discrepancy to be hidden.

⚡

Speed earns trust

A pilot that goes live in two weeks proves more than a six-month RFP process. We ship fast, show results early, and let the numbers make the case.

🔒

Data belongs to you

Your employees' skill data is yours - full stop. We process it, we never own it. No model training on customer data, ever.

🤝

Humans make the call

The engine recommends. The manager decides. We automate the reconciliation, not the judgment. Every disputed skill goes to a human for adjudication.
